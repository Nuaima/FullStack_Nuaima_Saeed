# Scoring Methodology

DealSignal uses a deterministic 100-point research-priority
framework.

## Operating History — 25 points

20+ years → 25
15–19 years → 22
10–14 years → 18
5–9 years → 12
<5 years → 5

## Capital Profile — 25 points

Reported funding → 10
Funding not reported → 12.5

## Company Size — 25 points

≤50 employees → 25
51–100 → 20
101–250 → 12
>250 → 5

## Digital Opportunity — 25 points

Uses a normalized digital-opportunity signal capped at 25.

## Why Deterministic?

The score should be:

- explainable
- reproducible
- auditable
- independent of LLM output

The LLM layer, in a production implementation, should explain
and synthesize evidence rather than determine the underlying score.
